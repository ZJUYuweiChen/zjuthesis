    \bfseries \zihao{3} 摘~要
\end{center}

拖延与注意缺陷多动障碍（ADHD）是大学生与年轻知识工作者中普遍存在的自我调节问题，传统干预手段往往难以触及任务启动困难背后的情绪与认知根源。本项目提出并实现了 Nudge，一种融合智能认知辅助与结构化任务管理的 AI 智能体驱动系统，其核心架构整合了结构化任务管理、基于 ReAct 推理循环的对话式 AI 智能体以及游戏化激励层三大模块。其中，智能体通过智能路由分类用户意图，运用结构化推理链分析任务厌恶、不确定感等拖延诱因，将复杂任务分解为可执行的微步骤并生成个性化、上下文感知的干预建议。实验评估表明，该智能体能够准确完成意图分类与工具委托，系统在并发负载下亦具备低延迟与高吞吐能力，验证了将大语言模型智能体推理与结构化任务管理、行为激励相结合的工程路径。通过交互式对话循环，智能体驱动的微干预将抽象的治疗策略转化为具体、即时的任务支持行动，有效降低任务启动门槛，为心理健康领域的认知辅助提供了一套可复用的后端智能体架构。

\begin{center}
    \bfseries \zihao{3} Abstract
\end{center}

Procrastination and Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) remain pervasive self-regulation challenges among college students and young knowledge workers, yet traditional interventions often fail to address the emotional and executive-function roots of task-initiation difficulties. This thesis presents Nudge, an AI-agent-driven task-management system designed to bridge this gap by combining intelligent cognitive assistance with structured task management. The core architecture integrates three modules: structured task management, a conversational AI agent powered by the ReAct reasoning loop, and a gamification layer. Specifically, the agent employs intelligent routing to classify user intent, then applies a structured reasoning chain to analyze procrastination triggers---such as task aversion and uncertainty---decomposing complex tasks into actionable micro-steps and generating personalized, context-aware nudges. Experimental evaluation demonstrates that the ReAct-based agent achieves accurate intent classification and robust tool delegation, while performance benchmarks confirm low end-to-end latency and high throughput under concurrent user load. This work validates a practical engineering pathway for integrating LLM-based agent reasoning with structured task management and behavioral incentives, where agent-driven micro-interventions lower task-initiation barriers by transforming abstract therapeutic strategies into concrete, real-time task-support actions through an interactive dialogue loop, ultimately presenting a reusable backend-oriented agentic architecture for cognitive-assistance systems in mental-health domains.
